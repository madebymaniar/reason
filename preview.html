Save something, let REASON suggest an intention from the surrounding context, and then search the archive using meaning instead of only names and dates.

personal archive

Memory, with meaning.

what this proves

A saved object can carry intention without becoming a task.

The prototype demonstrates capture → suggestion → storage → recall, while keeping the interaction deliberately lightweight.

what it does not prove yet

Whether intention can be inferred accurately enough to feel useful.

That is the real experiment: is a tiny layer of context more valuable than the friction required to preserve it?
